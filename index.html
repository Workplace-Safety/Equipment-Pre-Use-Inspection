1. General Condition & Safety Devices

Backup alarm/warning devices functional
Lights (headlights, taillights, warning lights) operational
Seat belt present and functional
Fire extinguisher mounted and charged (if required)
Rollover protection (ROPS/FOPS) intact, no damage
Horn/audible warning device works properly

2. Engine & Fluids

Engine oil level adequate, no leaks
Hydraulic fluid level adequate, no leaks
Coolant level adequate
Fuel level adequate for planned operation
No visible fuel, oil, or hydraulic leaks under equipment

3. Tires/Tracks & Brakes

Tires properly inflated, no cuts or excessive wear
Tracks tight and in good condition (if applicable)
Service brakes operate properly
Parking brake holds equipment securely

4. Controls & Hydraulics

All controls operate smoothly (steering, throttle, boom, bucket)
Hydraulic hoses intact, no bulges, cracks, or leaks
Boom, bucket, forks operate smoothly (no binding or jerking)
All safety lock-out controls function properly

5. Operator Station & Visibility

Windows/mirrors clean, no cracks obstructing visibility
Gauges/instruments readable and functioning
Operator manual/load chart accessible in cab (if required)
Cab/operator station clean and free of debris
⚠ DO NOT OPERATE equipment if any safety-critical deficiency is found. Tag out and notify maintenance/supervisor immediately.
